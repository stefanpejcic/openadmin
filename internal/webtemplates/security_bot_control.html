How each policy works

  • Allow — Lets known AI crawlers and search engines access the site normally.
  • Rate-limit (10/min) — Caps known AI crawlers at 10 requests/minute per IP (returns HTTP 429 when exceeded); normal visitors and search engines (Googlebot, Bingbot) are unaffected.
  • Block (403) — Immediately blocks known AI crawlers at Caddy with HTTP 403; normal visitors and search engines are unaffected.
  • Apply to all domains / Disable module — Bulk-applies the selected policy across all Caddy sites, or turns off the module and hides AI & bot control from OpenPanel users.
